# SocietyOS Engineering Knowledge Portal

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## Purpose

The SocietyOS Engineering Knowledge Portal is the permanent repository of engineering knowledge for the SocietyOS platform.

It exists to preserve the complete engineering understanding of every business capability implemented within SocietyOS.

The portal replaces conversational history with structured, version-controlled engineering knowledge.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

Knowledge is organized around **Society Operations**, not around source code.

Every Knowledge Article answers one primary question:

> **How does SocietyOS fulfil this user requirement?**

Technical implementation exists beneath the user requirement.

## Engineering Principles

- Business capability before implementation.
- Engineering truth before narrative.
- Executable evidence before description.
- One source of truth for every business capability.
- Permanent engineering knowledge.
